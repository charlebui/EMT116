# Anatomy matching provenance

13 matching sets, 141 labeled parts. This is separate practice and does not count as an MCQ or a completed knowledge-objective question in exam coverage.

Letter/name mapping was transcribed from the supplied Chapter 6 worksheet answer-key photos: IMG_1007(1).jpeg, IMG_1008(2).jpeg, IMG_1009(1).jpeg. The seven diagram photos are supplied worksheets; answer-key photos are not included in public assets.

Descriptions use the supplied Human Body Part I/II lecture transcripts and the location indicated on the supplied diagrams. When the lecture does not describe a detailed function, the description is limited to the pictured location or relationship rather than adding outside physiology. No outside medical sources were used.

The original key uses Maxilla (inferior skull view) and Maxillae (front view); both use the same upper-jaw choice to avoid testing singular/plural wording. Male structures repeated in front/side views deliberately permit repeated answers. Source spelling has been normalized for readability, with original term meanings preserved. Source page numbers are not shown to students.

Matching grades checked answers individually. Unanswered labels remain separate. Latest checked answers are retained locally per part, while drafts and new rounds do not erase previously recorded matching evidence. Matching evidence is independent of existing MCQ progress.

Verification: tests/anatomy.test.js checks all correct mappings, wrong and unanswered grading, duplicate-name matching, progress counts, and asset existence. Browser QA is unavailable in this environment; the diagrams remain supplied photographs, with a full-size image link for reading small labels.

## Diagram redraws

The built-in image-generation tool produced six white-background redraw assets covering 11 diagrams from the supplied worksheet photos. Male and female reproductive systems use separate source-guided SVG schematics with front and side views and lettered leader lines. Original photos remain linked for comparison.

The redraws are Generated illustrations reviewed against the supplied photos for label assignments, leader-line associations, grouped regions, and visible structure relationships. They are not independently medically validated or guaranteed pixel-identical. A first thumb-joint leader incorrectly terminated on a carpal and was regenerated to terminate at the thumb carpometacarpal joint. Airway leader positions for B, E, J and M were refined against the original photo. No outside medical sources were used.

Matching identifiers, answers, descriptions, and saved progress remain the same.

## Individual diagram framing

Each matching set defines a pixel-bounded viewport into its source image, rendered as an inline SVG image with viewBox clipping. This preserves the existing anatomy and labels without a further redraw. Shared-sheet regions do not overlap, and bounds retain the selected diagram's labels. Skull front/inferior views stay together; reproductive front/side views stay together for their respective systems. The enlargement page uses the same framing, rather than linking to a combined sheet. Original source photos remain accessible separately.
